# ARKO Corp

> ARKO Corp (Nasdaq: ARKO) is a Fortune 500 company and one of the largest operators of
> convenience stores and wholesalers of fuel in the United States. Through its subsidiary GPM
> Investments it operates roughly 1,400 company-operated stores under 25+ regional brands across
> 30+ states, plus proprietary cardlock fleet-fueling locations and a wholesale fuel distribution
> business. In 2026 ARKO completed the IPO of ARKO Petroleum Corp., which carries the wholesale,
> fleet fueling and petroleum distribution segments.

## Machine-readable API surface

**ARKO Corp publishes no API.** As of 2026-09-18 the company operates no developer program, no
developer portal, no SDK, and no machine-readable contract of any kind. This was established by
probe, not by assumption:

- No OpenAPI, Swagger, GraphQL SDL, AsyncAPI, MCP server or A2A agent card on any host the company
  controls: arkocorp.com, gpminvestments.com, fasrewards.com, arkopetroleum.com (each with and
  without `www.`).
- Every closed-list `/.well-known/` discovery path (security.txt, openid-configuration,
  oauth-authorization-server, oauth-protected-resource, api-catalog, ai-plugin.json, ucp.json,
  acp.json, aauth-resource.json, apis.json, agent-card.json, agent.json) plus `/apis.json`,
  `/apis.yml`, `/openapi.json`, `/openapi.yaml`, `/swagger.json`, `/api-docs` and `/llms.txt`
  returned 404 on all four hosts (see `well-known/arko-well-known.yml`).
- `fasrewards.com` and `arkopetroleum.com` use wildcard DNS, so `api.`, `developer.`, `docs.`
  and any other subdomain resolve — but they serve the same marketing page (fasrewards) or a
  bare 404 (arkopetroleum). No developer subdomain of `arkocorp.com` or `gpminvestments.com`
  resolves at all.
- `www.gpminvestments.com` is a WordPress install whose `/wp-json` endpoint serves the stock
  WordPress REST discovery document (wp/v2, oembed/1.0, elementor, wordfence, contact-form-7 and
  similar plugin namespaces). That is default CMS infrastructure, not a product API ARKO designed,
  documented or supports, so it is deliberately NOT catalogued as an ARKO API.
- The fas REWARDS loyalty program is delivered as a consumer mobile app (App Store / Google
  Play) with no public integration surface.

If you are an agent looking for a callable ARKO Corp API, there is not one to call.
